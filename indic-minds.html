Indic Minds · Season One: Nyaya

The Lost Art of Argument

Header image: Jānakīnātha Bhaṭṭācārya, Siddhāntamañjarī (Śabdapariccheda). Source: University of Pennsylvania, Kislak Center for Special Collections, Rare Books and Manuscripts, Ms. Coll. 390, Item 2106. Public domain.

Indic Minds explores how the Nyaya school approached knowledge, reasoning, argument, evidence, disagreement, and the discipline of correcting oneself, read in comparative conversation with Aristotle's own logical and rhetorical works.

The recurring question beneath every episode is not simply who is reasoning badly, but whether the viewer, and the presenter, remain willing to discover they are wrong.

A note on scope. Indic Minds is distinct from the Indic Wisdom series on this site. Indic Wisdom asks what classical Indic ideas can illuminate about contemporary human problems. Indic Minds asks a narrower question: how should a disciplined mind know, reason, argue, and correct itself?

Its first season works through the Nyaya Sutra specifically; it is not a general survey of Indian philosophy.

Episode 1 is published. Episodes 2 through 10 are planned and will be added as they are scripted, verified against source translations, and recorded.
