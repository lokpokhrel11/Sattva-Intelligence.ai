Attention, Action, and the Good Life

University of Victoria Continuing Studies · Scheduled to begin March 2027

This course examines attention, judgment, action, and the good life through classical Indic philosophy, with particular attention to Sāṃkhya, Yoga, and the Bhagavad Gītā. It connects careful philosophical interpretation with contemporary questions about how we direct attention, evaluate choices, and act responsibly.

Teaching resources and independent study

How to use these materials

Sattva Intelligence is an independent, open-access companion to the study of classical Indic texts and philosophical traditions. Instructors may select individual episodes, passages, and explanatory resources to supplement assigned primary readings. Students can move between the narrative context, Sanskrit forms, and thematic indexes. These pathways are suggestions, not complete courses or accredited instructional modules.

A practical classroom approach

Choose a primary passage from the assigned text, use an episode or study note to establish context, compare the original wording with a scholarly translation, and discuss which conclusions are grounded in the passage and which are interpretive. Students should cite the primary text and relevant scholarly editions or translations, not rely on this website alone.
