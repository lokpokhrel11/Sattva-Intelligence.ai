Bhagavata Purana · Episode 13

Vidura’s Questions: Creation, Bondage, and Fearlessness

Canto 3, Chapters 5–7

Watch Episode 13 Episode Index

Episode Overview

Why do people pursue happiness yet remain caught in suffering? Vidura’s questions to Maitreya open a discussion of creation, the living being’s bondage, māyā, and the spiritual knowledge that offers freedom from fear.

Text and Source

Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.5–7. Key passages include 3.5.2 and 3.7.41. The principal English reference for this reading is G. V. Tagare’s translation (Motilal Banarsidass).
