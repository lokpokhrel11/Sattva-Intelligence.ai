The Sattva Intelligence project discusses classical Sanskrit texts, including the Bhagavata Purana, Bhagavad Gita, Yoga Sutra, Upanishads, and other works from Indic textual traditions.

The underlying classical texts may be in the public domain depending on the particular work and edition. Modern translations, editions, commentaries, introductions, annotations, and editorial materials may remain protected by copyright.
