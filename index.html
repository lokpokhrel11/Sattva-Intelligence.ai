A long-term critical reading project

Sattva Intelligence

Sattva Intelligence is a long-term reading of the Bhagavata Purana and other classical Indic texts, approached as philosophy, ethics, and psychology rather than theology or devotional practice. This is not a project about faith. It reads these texts the way one might read Aristotle or the Stoics: carefully, with named sources, and with corrections made publicly when something is wrong.

Teaching and independent study

For University Teaching and Independent Study

Sattva Intelligence offers open-access resources for students, instructors, and researchers studying the Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Sanskrit textual interpretation, and Indic philosophical traditions. Its episode discussions, selected Sanskrit passages, grammatical notes, and research indexes can support university teaching and independent study as supplementary materials.
