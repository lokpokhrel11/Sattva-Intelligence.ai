Sattva Intelligence

Sattva Intelligence brings close readings of classical Indic texts into conversation with original research in philosophy, ethics, communication, and human judgment. The project studies philosophical, theological, and devotional arguments in their textual contexts, without requiring readers to share particular religious commitments. Sources, interpretations, uncertainties, and corrections are identified openly.

Teaching and independent study

For University Teaching and Independent Study

Sattva Intelligence offers open-access resources for students, instructors, and researchers studying the Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Sanskrit textual interpretation, and Indic philosophical traditions. Its episode discussions, selected Sanskrit passages, grammatical notes, and research indexes can support university teaching and independent study as supplementary materials.
