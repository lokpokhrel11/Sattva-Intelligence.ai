Concept Index

A growing index of philosophical, ethical, and textual concepts discussed across the Bhagavata Purana series. Entries identify episodes and passages in which a concept receives substantive treatment.

Return to Episode Index

How to Use This Index

The Episode Index follows the Bhagavata Purana chapter by chapter. This Concept Index provides another way into the series. It allows readers, students, and researchers to follow philosophical and ethical questions across different episodes and textual settings.

Entries are included where a concept receives substantive discussion, rather than every time a term happens to appear. Where useful, the index also identifies cross-textual comparison with traditions such as classical Samkhya, the Bhagavad Gita, and the Yoga Sutra.

For Episodes 1–7, links currently lead to the complete episode. From Episode 8 onward, precise timestamps are provided where available. Earlier entries may be given more precise timestamps in the future.

A

Ashvamedha

Episode 10 — Canto 1, Chapter 12
Yudhishthira's postwar sacrifices, the depleted treasury, and the wealth associated with King Marutta. The passage forms part of the larger movement from war toward restored rule.

D

Death / Mortality

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapters 16–19
Parikshit learns that he has seven days to live. The episode examines how the knowledge of approaching death reorganizes his priorities and leads him to ask what is worth hearing, knowing, and doing when time is limited.

Dharma

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapters 16–19
Dharma appears in the form of the bull whose four legs are austerity, purity, compassion, and truth. The episode examines the damage associated with Kali and Parikshit's responsibility to restrain wrongdoing and restore what has been damaged.

Watch Episode 1

G

Guna / Triguna

Episode 10 — Canto 1, Chapter 10
Discussion of sattva, rajas, and tamas in relation to Prakriti and manifestation. The episode distinguishes this primarily cosmological use from later or more familiar uses of the gunas as descriptions of human disposition.

J

Judgment / Acting Before Understanding

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapters 18–19
Shringi hears a report about Parikshit's conduct, does not verify what happened, and imposes a fatal curse. His father, Shamika, judges the punishment disproportionate. The episode uses the scene to examine rapid judgment based on incomplete information.

Watch Episode 1

K

Kali / Kali Yuga

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapters 16–17
Kali is presented as the spirit of the age. The episode examines Parikshit's encounter with Kali and the assigned places associated with gambling, drinking, sexual exploitation, slaughter, and gold.

Name and form in Bhagavata Purana 1.10.22, discussed in relation to Prakriti and the emergence of a differentiated world.

Parikshit's prenatal vision, birth, predicted future, naming, and search for the figure he had seen before birth. The episode connects this beginning of his life with the questions he will later ask as death approaches.

Power and Knowledge

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapter 19
Parikshit leaves royal power behind and bows before Shuka. The episode uses this scene to distinguish political authority from knowledge and to examine the willingness to learn from someone outside ordinary structures of status and power.

Bhagavata Purana 1.10.22 and the relationship between Prakriti, manifestation, and Krishna's power. The discussion compares the passage with classical Samkhya while preserving the Bhagavata's own conceptual framework.

Presence and Absence

Episode 10 — Canto 1, Chapters 10–11
Krishna's departure from Hastinapura and return to Dvaraka are examined as two relational perspectives on the same journey: separation for one community and homecoming for another.

Purusha

Episode 10 — Canto 1, Chapter 10
Bhagavata Purana 1.10.21 describes Krishna as Purushah Puratanah. The episode compares this usage with the Purusha–Prakriti distinction of classical Samkhya and explains why shared Sanskrit terminology should not automatically be assumed to have an identical technical function across texts.

Responsibility

Episode 10 — Canto 1, Chapter 10
Yudhishthira returns to rule after Bhishma's death. The discussion considers the movement from responsibility for an irreversible past toward responsibility for action in the present.

Samkhya

Episode 10 — Canto 1, Chapter 10
An extended comparison of Purusha, Prakriti, and the three gunas in classical Samkhya with the related vocabulary of Bhagavata Purana 1.10.21–22. The comparison emphasizes both conceptual overlap and philosophical difference.

Seeing and Searching

Episode 10 — Canto 1, Chapter 12
Parikshit sees the figure who protects him before birth. Bhagavata Purana 1.12.30 later describes him examining or looking among people for the one he had previously seen. The episode places this early search alongside the questions Parikshit will ask near the end of his life.

Shravana / Listening

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapter 19
After learning that he has seven days to live, Parikshit gives up his kingdom, goes to the Ganges, and chooses to spend his remaining time listening. Shuka's arrival establishes listening as the setting through which the Bhagavata's teaching will unfold.

V

Vairagya

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapters 18–19
Vairagya is discussed as non-attachment rather than indifference. The episode compares the term with its technical use in the Yoga Sutra and connects it with Parikshit's changed relationship to kingdom, wealth, status, and mortality.

Watch Episode 1

Viveka

Episode 1 — Canto 1, Chapters 18–19
Viveka is examined as discriminative discernment, including comparison with its more technical uses in Yoga and Vedanta. The episode applies the concept to Parikshit's recognition that mortality changes the relative importance of power, possession, and status.
