How to Use This Index

The Verse Index provides a textual route through the series. It identifies selected Bhagavata Purana verses and passages that receive substantive discussion and connects them with the relevant episode and scholarly companion page.

This is not a concordance of every verse mentioned or narrated in the series. A verse or passage is included when it receives significant textual, philosophical, ethical, linguistic, or interpretive attention.

Some entries identify a range of verses rather than a single verse. This is done where the episode treats the passage as a connected textual unit.

The index is cumulative. Earlier episodes may receive additional entries as their research records are further verified. Exact verse references are not added where the surviving research record does not establish them with sufficient confidence.

About Coverage

The absence of an episode or verse from this index does not mean that the passage is unimportant or that it was not covered in the series. The index records passages for which an exact textual reference has been verified and which received substantive attention in the episode.

Episode 4, for example, gives sustained attention to Brahma's instruction, practice, vision, and the four foundational verses of Canto 2, Chapter 9. Exact entries will be added after the relevant verse references are verified against the episode's research record.

Related Research Paths

The Sattva Intelligence Bhagavata Purana archive can be approached through several complementary research paths.

Episode Index: follow the series sequentially by canto and chapter.

Concept Index: follow philosophical, ethical, and textual concepts across different episodes.

Verse Index: begin with a specific Bhagavata Purana verse or passage and locate the episode and research material in which it receives substantive discussion.

Sanskrit Terms: follow significant Sanskrit vocabulary across episodes and textual contexts.

Sanskrit Text & Grammar: examine selected verses through padaccheda, morphology, syntax, and close attention to how the Sanskrit affects interpretation.
